WHY NO src/lib/accounts/*.ts FIX WAS NEEDED
Before writing these tests I specifically checked whether recordBillPayment() (payables.ts)
and applyDebitNoteToBill() (debitNotes.ts) have a TOCTOU race on a bill's remaining-payable
cap, since neither uses the atomic INSERT...SELECT...WHERE balance guard that
creditNotes.ts/debitNotes.ts use for their own note balances. They don't need one:
db/client.ts's runInTenantTransaction() takes pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended(orgId))
as the FIRST statement of every tenant transaction and holds it until commit, so two
concurrent writes for the same org are fully serialized by Postgres itself before either one
re-reads anything — the second transaction only starts executing once the first has
committed (or rolled back), so it always sees fresh state. The "concurrent cash payment and
DN-apply stay within the bill's remaining payable" test above is exactly the real-DB proof
of this: it genuinely races the two real code paths against a real Postgres instance and
confirms the invariant holds. This was worth checking and recording, not assuming.
